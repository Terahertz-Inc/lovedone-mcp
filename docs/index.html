LovedOne Family and Soft, for agents

This GitHub Pages site is the public front door for LovedOne’s Model Context Protocol servers. The product itself — the family inbox and the care-agency workspace — lives on the official website.

Go to lovedone.app

  • LovedOne Family

    One household. An AI client can read that family’s inbox and care records, invite siblings, and send replies — never another family’s data.

    Spec: docs.lovedone.app/mcp-family

  • LovedOne Soft

    One care agency. An AI client can read that agency’s clients, visits, and invoices, log notes, and schedule visits — without collecting family keys.

    Spec: docs.lovedone.app/mcp-vendor

Registry listings: Terahertz-Inc/lovedone-mcp. Product: lovedone.app.
